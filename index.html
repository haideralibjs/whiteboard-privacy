Whiteboard — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy applies to the Whiteboard — Draw & Teach Android app (package com.boardstudio.app), published by Haider Sarfraz ("we", "us").

Summary

Whiteboard does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything you create stays on your device.

Data we do not collect

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers or usage analytics.
  • The app contains no advertising and no third-party tracking or analytics SDKs.

Data stored on your device

Your boards, drawings, text, photos, videos, stickers and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded to our servers. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

Photos and media

When you choose a photo or video, the app reads only the item you select. Background removal and sticker creation run entirely on your device using on-device machine learning; photos are not sent anywhere.

Exporting and sharing

If you export a board as PNG or PDF, the file is saved or shared only where you choose, using Android's standard share and save dialogs.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
